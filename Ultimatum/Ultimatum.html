THE BRONZE COLOSSUS
Phase 1
A/D or Arrow Keys: Move · Space: Jump · E: Attack · Q: Dodge · R: Restart

⚔ Controls

  • A / ◀ or ← Arrow – Move left
  • D / ▶ or → Arrow – Move right
  • Space / JUMP – Jump (double-tap to double-jump)
  • E / ATTACK – Attack enemy (costs stamina)
  • Q / DODGE – Dodge incoming attacks (costs energy)
  • R – Restart after game over
